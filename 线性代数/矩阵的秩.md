## 分块矩阵的秩

1. $r(A+B)\leqslant r(A,B)\leqslant r(A)+r(B)$
	$r(A+B)\leqslant r\left(\begin{matrix}A\\B\end{matrix}\right)\leqslant r(A)+r(B)$
2. A可逆, 则$r\left(\begin{matrix}A&O\\C&D\end{matrix}\right)=r(A)+r(D)$
	$r\left(\begin{matrix}A&B\\O&D\end{matrix}\right)=r(A)+r(D)$
3. $r\left(\begin{matrix}A&O\\O&B\end{matrix}\right)=r\left(\begin{matrix}O&A\\B&O\end{matrix}\right)=r(A)+r(B)$

	$r\left(\begin{matrix}A&B\\O&D\end{matrix}\right)\geqslant r(A)+r(D)$

## 秩1矩阵的性质

非零向量$\alpha=\left(\begin{matrix}a_{1}\\a_{2}\end{matrix}\right),\beta=\left(\begin{matrix}b_{1}\\b_{2}\end{matrix}\right)$
1. $\alpha^{\top}\beta=\left(\begin{matrix}a_{1}&a_{2}\end{matrix}\right)\left(\begin{matrix}b_{1}\\b_{2}\end{matrix}\right)=a_{1}b_{1}+a_{2}b_{2}$
    $\beta^{\top}\alpha=\left(\begin{matrix}b_{1}&b_{2}\end{matrix}\right)\left(\begin{matrix}a_{1}\\a_{2}\end{matrix}\right)=a_{1}b_{1}+a_{2}b_{2}$
	$(\alpha^\top\beta)^\top=\beta^\top\alpha=\alpha^\top\beta$
	$(\alpha,\beta)=\alpha\beta^{\top}=\beta\alpha^{\top}$
2. $\alpha\beta^{\top}=\left(\begin{matrix}a_{1}\\a_{2}\end{matrix}\right)\left(\begin{matrix}b_{1}&b_{2}\end{matrix}\right)=\left(\begin{matrix}a_{1}b_{1}&a_{1}b_{2}\\a_{2}b_{1}&a_{2}b_{2}\end{matrix}\right)=A$
	第二行=第一行$\times \frac{a_{1}}{a_{2}}\implies r(A)=1$
	$\beta\alpha^{\top}=(\alpha \beta^{\top})^\top=\left(\begin{matrix}a_{1}b_{1}&a_{2}b_{1}\\a_{1}b_{2}&a_{2}b_{2}\end{matrix}\right)=B$
	$r(B)=r(A^\top)=r(A)=1$

>[!attention] 任何秩为1的矩阵可以转化为一个列向量乘以一个行向量

>[!attention] $tr(A)=\beta^\top\alpha=\alpha^\top\beta$

n阶秩1矩阵$A=\alpha^\top\beta$
$A^2=\alpha^\top\beta\alpha^\top\beta=\alpha^\top tr(A)\beta=tr(A)\alpha^\top\beta=tr(A)A$

>[!attention] 秩1矩阵的n次方=$tr(A)^{n-1}A$

$$
\begin{align}
&A\alpha=\lambda \alpha\implies A^2\alpha=\lambda A\alpha=\lambda^2\alpha\\
&A^2\alpha=tr(A)A\alpha=tr(A)\lambda \alpha \\
&\lambda^2\alpha=tr(A)\lambda \alpha\implies \lambda[\lambda-tr(A)]\alpha=0 \\
&\alpha\neq_{0}\implies \lambda[\lambda-tr(A)]=0\implies \lambda=0或\lambda=tr(A)
\end{align}
$$

n阶A有n个特征值$\lambda_{i}(i=1,2,\dots,n)$
1. $|A|=\prod^n_{i=1}\lambda_{i}$
2. $tr(A)=\sum^n_{{i=1}}\lambda_{i}$

>[!attention] r(A)=1, $\lambda_{1}=tr(A),\lambda_{2}=\dots=\lambda_{n}=0$
>$B=A+kE\implies\lambda_{1}=tr(A)+k,\lambda_{2}=\dots\lambda_{n}=k\implies|B|=(tr(A)+k)k^{n-1}$
