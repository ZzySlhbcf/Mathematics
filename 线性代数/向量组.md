+ 对于任意一组实数$k_{1},k_{2},\dots k_{n}$, $k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n}$称为$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$的一个线性组合

>[!info] 给定向量的**张成空间**: 所有可以表示为给定向量线性组合的向量集合

+ 对于$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n},\beta$, 若存在一组实数$k_{1},\dots k_{n}$, 使$\beta=k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n}$, 称$\beta$由$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性表出

>[!note] 线性相关与线性无关
> + $\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性相关$\Longleftrightarrow$存在不全为0的数$k_{1},k_{2},\dots k_{n}$使得$k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n}=0$
> + $\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性无关$\Longleftrightarrow$若$k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}+\dots+k_{n}\alpha_{n}=0$, 则$k_{1}=k_{2}=\dots=k_{n}=0$

+ 若$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性相关$\Longleftrightarrow\exists \alpha_{i}$可由余下n-1个向量线性表出

1. 往一个向量组里添加向量提高相关性, 减少向量提高无关性
2. 若向量个数高于维数则一定线性相关, 即n+1个n维向量必线性相关
3. 若$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性无关, 但$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n},\beta$线性相关$\implies\beta$可由$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性表出

>[!info] 向量空间的一个**基**是张成该空间的一个**线性无关**向量集

>[!note] 极大线性无关组
> 若在$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$中选出$r$个向量$\alpha_{n_{1}},\dots,\alpha_{n_{r}}$线性无关, $\forall(r+1)$个向量都线性相关, 则称$\alpha_{n_{1}},\dots,\alpha_{n_{r}}$为$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$的极大线性无关组
> + 若$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性无关, 则$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$就是自己的极大无关组

>[!note] 向量组$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$的秩
> $\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$的极大无关组中向量$\alpha_{n_{1}},\dots,\alpha_{n_{r}}$的个数r, 写作$r(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n})=r$

+ $\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$中任一向量都可由其极大线性无关组$\alpha_{n_{1}},\dots,\alpha_{n_{r}}$线性表出
+ $\alpha_{n_{1}},\dots,\alpha_{n_{r}}$也可由$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性表出

能表出则向量空间维度更大, 而秩代表线性变换所能覆盖的空间维度, 所以能标出别的, 自己的秩不会比对方小
+ $\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$可由$\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s}$线性表出$\implies r(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n})\leqslant r(\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s})$
+ $\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$与$\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s}$等价$\implies r(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n})=r(\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s})$
	$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$与$\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s}$可互相线性表出
+ $r(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n})=r(\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s})$, 且$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$可由$\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s}$线性表出$\implies\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$与$\beta_{1},\dots,\beta_{s}$等价

1. 单个非零向量线性无关
2. 零向量作为向量组时线性相关

+ $\beta$可由$\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}$线性表出$\Longleftrightarrow r(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n})=r(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n},\beta)$, 即加入$\beta$秩不变
